Part two

what's going on out there for singles.

the singles events are 90% women and everyone noticed. a clip went around this week about men not showing up to singles events, and the internet had 10,000 comments worth of feelings about it. the theory that kept surfacing: the men doing fine on the apps have no reason to buy a ticket, and the ones who aren't assume a room won't go better than a screen. so nobody comes, and a hundred women stand around with drink tickets.

but here's the part i actually care about. the events that did work in those comments weren't mixers at all. they were the ones where you're doing something. a group hike. trivia. anything where you rotate and talk to people because the activity makes you. standing in a bar hoping is not a plan. a group hike is a plan.

filing this under: the answer was never the mixer. the answer was having somewhere to be.

 

Part three

i read everything in the group chat this week, and three things came up over and over.

two. most of you aren't here for dating. you're here for friends. i went in expecting a dating community and what i actually have is women trying to make real friends as adults, which is somehow the harder ask.

three. the wins channel is unhinged in the best way. quitting the toxic job. the therapy breakthrough. painting one wall of a new apartment. also a truly staggering number of dogs. someone has formally requested a pets channel and honestly she's right.
